# Asset attribution

## Project-owned assets

ATP Insight brand marks, interface screenshots, social preview images, hero media and abstract surface-court fallbacks were created for this project. They are included for presentation of the deployed portfolio product. Their presence does not grant a general reuse license.

## Country flags

Country-flag raster assets are used only as small nationality indicators in the interface. Their exact upstream package provenance should be confirmed before changing repository visibility. If that provenance cannot be established, the recommended fallback is to replace them with Unicode regional-indicator flags or a clearly licensed flag set.

## Third-party names and marks

ATP, tournament and player names are used descriptively. No affiliation with, endorsement by or official status from the ATP Tour or individual tournaments is claimed.
