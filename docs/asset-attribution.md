# Asset attribution

## Project-owned assets

ATP Insight brand marks, interface screenshots, social preview images, hero media and abstract surface-court fallbacks were created for this project. They are included for presentation of the deployed portfolio product. Their presence does not grant a general reuse license.

## Tournament venue photographs

The public catalogue covers all 66 known ATP event IDs across the available 2025 and 2026 editions. It contains specific photographs for **49 events** (48 distinct photographs, because the two Davis Cup qualifying stages deliberately share one competition photo). The remaining 17 events retain labelled surface fallbacks; their individual research limitations are recorded in [Tournament year and image catalogue](torneos-anio-imagenes.md). The previous release contained two specific photographs.

Sources were checked on 3 October 2026. New photographs are served locally from `web/public/images/tournaments/venues/` as WebP, at no more than 1280 pixels wide, without upscaling. Existing Båstad and Umag assets remain in `courts/`. No additional private images from that directory are included. All derivatives of CC BY-SA photographs remain under the **same CC BY-SA license** as their respective originals; CC BY derivatives remain under the listed CC BY terms. These licenses apply to those photographs, not to project code or branding. Each displayed photograph links to its source and license record.

The pictures identify the venue or competition; they do not claim to depict every edition. Exterior photographs are used where a suitable licensed court view was unavailable. Dallas is cropped to the Ford Center facade and Hangzhou to the lotus tennis arena. The Davis Cup and Laver Cup photos represent those competitions, whose host venue can vary. United Cup uses its Perth venue. No photo of another tournament is used as a fallback.

## Country flags

Country-flag raster assets are used only as small nationality indicators in the interface. Their exact upstream package provenance should be confirmed before changing repository visibility. If that provenance cannot be established, the recommended fallback is to replace them with Unicode regional-indicator flags or a clearly licensed flag set.

## Third-party names and marks

ATP, tournament and player names are used descriptively. No affiliation with, endorsement by or official status from the ATP Tour or individual tournaments is claimed.
